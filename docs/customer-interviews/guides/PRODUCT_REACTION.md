# PRODUCT REACTION guide (separate phase)

**Only after PROBLEM DISCOVERY is complete** and notes for pain/frequency/alternatives are captured.

**Still not a pitch.** You may show Capital (cold Harbor path or a short clip). You observe reaction and ask about **behavior and understanding**, not purchase intent theater.

---

## Entry line

> “Thanks — I’m going to shift gears. I’ll show you something related to money learning. I’m not selling it today. I want your honest reactions. Please think aloud.”

Do **not** say: “This is the solution to what you just described.”

---

## Reaction prompts (non-leading)

1. “What is this, in your own words?”  
2. “What does it remind you of that you’ve used before?”  
3. “Where did you hesitate or get lost?”  
4. “What felt familiar from the situations you told me about earlier — if anything?”  
5. “What feels irrelevant to the problems you described?”  
6. “If you put this in front of [your child / students] tomorrow, what do you think would happen?”  
7. “What would stop you from trying this even once?”  
8. “Without naming a price — have you paid for anything in this category before? How did that decision get made?”  

### Explicitly avoid

- “Would you use Capital?”  
- “Wouldn’t this be useful for what you just said?”  
- “Would you pay $10 / $40 / our price?”  
- “Most parents love the Plinth — don’t you?”

### If they volunteer willingness to pay

Record as **customer language** + context. Do **not** negotiate or anchor. Follow with: “Tell me about a time you actually paid for something like that — what happened after?”

---

## Usability handoff

If the session includes observational tasks (UT-01…), switch to `docs/usability-research/FACILITATOR_PROTOCOL.md` and **stop** interview-style persuasion. Score success/fail separately from problem evidence.

---

## Close PRODUCT REACTION

- Note mismatches: their stated problem vs what they attended to in the product.  
- Note features ignored vs unexpectedly valued (behavior, not compliments).  
- Do not update ICP from one reaction session.
