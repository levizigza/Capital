# Segment-specific probes

Tied to discovery hypotheses (`H-SEG-*`, `IH-*`). Still **behavior-first**. Use after the core PROBLEM DISCOVERY list.

---

## S1 — Parents + kids 6–11 (Round A)

Hypotheses in play: H-SEG-01, H-PAIN-01/02, IH-1, IH-2, IH-8.

- “Tell me about the last time money came up with your 7–10-year-old.”  
- “What did you do in that moment?”  
- “What was difficult?”  
- “What have you already tried to teach saving or spending?”  
- “Do you use an allowance app or card? Tell me about the last week with it — what it did and didn’t do.”  
- “Have you ever paid for a book, app, course, or kit for kid money skills? What happened?”  
- “Who decides on kids’ apps and games in your house?”  
- “What happens if you don’t get better at teaching this over the next year?”

**Product reaction (later):** watch for rails-must-have vs fiction-OK *in their words*; do not ask “Would you use Capital?”

## S2 — Homeschool (Round B)

H-SEG-05, IH-4.

- “Tell me about the last life-skills unit you taught.”  
- “How did you pick that curriculum?”  
- “What was difficult about finishing it?”  
- “What does ‘done’ look like for your records?”  
- “Have you paid for curriculum packs in the last year? Which, and did you renew?”  
- “How often do you rethink tools mid-term?”

---

## S4 — Teachers (Round C)

H-SEG-03, IH-5.

- “Tell me about the last money or personal-finance lesson you taught.”  
- “What did students do? What flopped?”  
- “What have you already tried (worksheets, NGPF, games, bank visits)?”  
- “Who paid or approved that?”  
- “What happens if engagement stays low?”  
- “How often does this unit run?”

---

## S3 — Youth org / CU education (Round D)

H-SEG-07.

- “Tell me about the last youth money workshop or series you ran.”  
- “How did you measure success?”  
- “What tools did you use? What got dropped?”  
- “Walk me through how a new tool would get funded.”

---

## S5 — Teens via parent gate (Round E)

H-SEG-02, IH-6. Interview **parent first** on discovery; teen product reaction only with assent.

Parent: “Tell me about the last time your teen handled their own money.”  
Teen (reaction phase): “Show me how you’d figure out what this is — I’m not going to explain it.”

---

## S7 — Solo adults (Round F)

H-SEG-04, IH-7, H-PAIN-04.

- “Tell me about the last time you tried to get a grip on spending or budgeting.”  
- “What did you open or do?”  
- “What have you already abandoned?”  
- “What did that cost?”  
- “When you’re stressed about money, do you want something playful — tell me about a real night, not a preference.”

---

## S6 — Grandparents (Round G)

H-SEG-06.

- “Tell me about the last educational gift you bought a grandchild.”  
- “What happened after you gave it?”  
- “Have you paid for classes, books, or apps for them before?”  
- “Who sets up tech in that household?”

---

## S8 — Coaches

H-SEG-08.

- “Tell me about the last homework you assigned a client between sessions.”  
- “What completion looked like.”  
- “What you’ve already tried that clients ignore.”
