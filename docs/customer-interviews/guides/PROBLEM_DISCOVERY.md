# PROBLEM DISCOVERY guide

**Mode:** Capital is **not** on screen. No feature tour.

Use open follow-ups: “Tell me more.” “What happened next?” “How did that feel?”

---

## Opening (all segments)

1. “To start, I’d love to hear how money comes up in your [home / classroom / program / life].”  
2. “Tell me about the **last time** money was a topic with [your child / students / yourself]. What happened?”  
3. “What did you do?”  
4. “What was difficult about that?”  
5. “What have you already tried?”  
6. “What did that cost you — money, time, stress, missed chances, or mistakes?”  
7. “What happens if you don’t solve this?”  
8. “How often does this happen?”  
9. “Have you ever **paid** for something that was supposed to help with this? What was it, and what happened?”

---

## Dig into alternatives (still no Capital)

10. “Walk me through the tool or approach you use most often now.”  
11. “When did you last stop using something you tried? Why?”  
12. “What almost worked?”  
13. “Who else is involved when you pick tools — partner, school, PTA, boss?”

---

## Decision-maker & barriers

14. “Who usually decides whether to buy or keep something like this?”  
15. “What would make you stick with a bad-fit tool longer than you should?”  
16. “What would make you refuse a tool even if the idea sounded good?”

---

## Desired outcome (in their words)

17. “If this problem were handled well six months from now, what would be different in day-to-day life?”  
18. “What words would you use to describe what you want? (I’ll write them down exactly.)”

---

## Optional depth (parents / money-with-kids)

- “Tell me about the last time your child wanted to buy something and you said no — or yes.”  
- “Do you move real money for kids (allowance apps, cards, cash jars)? What does that solve, and what does it still miss?”  
- “Have you ever tried to teach a money lesson that didn’t stick? What did you try?”

## Optional depth (homeschool)

- “Tell me about the last life-skills or money unit you ran. How did you choose it?”  
- “What does ‘done’ look like for your records?”

## Optional depth (teachers / programs)

- “Tell me about the last personal-finance or money-math unit you taught.”  
- “How did that get approved or paid for?”  
- “What failed with student engagement or privacy rules?”

## Optional depth (solo adults)

- “Tell me about the last time you tried a budget or money app. What happened?”  
- “When money stress hits, what do you actually open on your phone?”

---

## Forbidden in this phase

- “Would you use Capital?”  
- “Wouldn’t a game be useful?”  
- “Would you pay $X?”  
- Showing Harbor / Cove / marketing site
