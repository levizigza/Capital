# Capital — one-page cold session (kid + parent observer)

**Use when:** Pattern #94 human playtest (later — not required for story/gameplay craft passes).  
**Time:** ~25–35 minutes · **Profile:** Fresh save · **Rule:** Observer does not hint unless player is stuck 3+ minutes.

Copy answers into [`docs/pattern-human-playtest.md`](../pattern-human-playtest.md) when done.

---

## Before you start

| Who | Do this |
|-----|---------|
| **Parent / observer** | Open Capital on a laptop or tablet. Start a **new profile**. Have this sheet + a pen ready. |
| **Kid (player)** | “You’re exploring a money island game. I’ll watch — ask me only if you’re really stuck.” |

**Do not say:** “save like Cove,” “pick the jar,” “Credit unlocks after Paycheck,” or any quest walkthrough.

---

## Session path (single sitting)

| Step | Where | What to watch | Observer notes (1 line) |
|------|-------|---------------|-------------------------|
| 1 | **Harbor** | Walk plaza, meet Piggy if prompted, reach Carpet Dock | Did they orient without you naming the goal? |
| 2 | **Coincraft Cove** | First painting — earn coins, talk to NPCs, reach **jar vs treat** choice | Did they understand it was *their* choice that sticks? |
| 3 | **Return Harbor** | Quiet ride home, Piggy / Plinth if shown | Did Harbor “feel” different to them (in their words)? |
| 4 | **Paycheck Peninsula** | **Stop talking.** Vendor Vee stall — two prices | **ITR check:** Did they choose without asking what Cove “meant”? (Y/N) |
| 5 | **Optional** | One side shore from the map (any era island) | Did they find a minigame and finish or bail? |
| 6 | **Stop** | Ask the six questions below — kid answers in their own words | |

---

## Six questions → #94 log

Ask aloud. Write short answers in [`pattern-human-playtest.md`](../pattern-human-playtest.md).

1. **Did you misunderstand what to do?** (Where? When?)
2. **Did anything feel unfair?** (What happened?)
3. **Did anything feel repetitive without a new beat?** (Which part?)
4. **Did the game ignore something you already learned?** (Example?)
5. **Did you get lost** — not knowing where to go or what the goal was?
6. **Was it fun, or only like homework?** (Would you play again?)

**Kid one-liner (optional):** “What is Capital about, in one sentence?”
