# Mural thesis — Capital’s irreducible metaphor

**Law:** If a feature cannot name its **money organ** and **suit verb**, it does not ship on the iconic spine.

## The mural (one sentence)

> You are inside living money. Harbor remembers. Cove holds. Paycheck clocks. Credit spirals.

Astro’s Playroom: you are inside the PS5; worlds are console organs; bots are its people.  
Capital: you are inside living money; islands are money organs; mascots are its temperaments.

## Organs (frozen triangle + Harbor)

| Organ | Island | Suit | Verbs | Must-feel |
|-------|--------|------|-------|-----------|
| **Memory** | Harbor Haven | Remember | Walk · Talk · Return | Home changed by a choice elsewhere |
| **Coin** | Coincraft Cove | Hold | Hold · Take · Hush | Irreversible Take — Harbor felt it |
| **Clock** | Paycheck Peninsula | Earn | Earn · Stamp · Shelter | Payday vs rainy-day loft |
| **Spiral** | Credit Kingdom | Borrow | Borrow · Weigh · Withstand | Interest as gravity |

Code source of truth: [`src/islands/moneyOrgans.ts`](../src/islands/moneyOrgans.ts).

## Piece checklist (every prop / NPC / portal)

1. Which organ?  
2. What verb does it teach or reward?  
3. Does poking it answer (toy culture)?  
4. Does returning home change because of it?  
5. Can a child name it after one session?

## Anti-patterns

- Genre cities that are not money organs  
- Shared shore kits as “done” without organ silhouette  
- HUD tips instead of Harbor memory  
- Mesh polish before a fun verb  
- New outer islands before Coin / Clock / Spiral each pass the cold retell test
