# Stripe cash timing — Canada

**Source (official):** [Stripe Payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts) (country table includes Canada).

Use this for **cash planning**, not for inventing revenue.

| Item | Stripe guidance (Canada) |
|------|---------------------------|
| Initial settlement timing | **7 calendar days** |
| Default subsequent settlement timing | **3 business days** |
| First live payout (common) | Stripe typically schedules the **initial payout ~7–14 days** after the first successful live payment; industry/risk can make the first payout longer |

### Planning implications for Capital

1. **First live Payment Link success ≠ money in the bank that day.**  
2. Budget operating cash through at least the **first 7–14 day** window after the first live charge.  
3. After the initial period, assume **~3 business days** settlement unless the Dashboard schedule says otherwise.  
4. Keep **test mode** for rehearsals; only founder-approved live charges count toward the one-stranger milestone’s “willingly give us money.”

Re-check [docs.stripe.com/payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts) before live launch — schedules can change by country and account.
