# Capital — refund policy (Phase 1 founding)

**Product:** Capital founding family / household access (digital adventure game).  
**Not:** a bank, debit card, or subscription (unless a later offer says otherwise).

## Refunds (founding one-time)

If you purchased the **founding one-time** access:

- You may request a refund within **14 days** of purchase if you cannot complete the **Cove Take** moment (the first irreversible money choice) due to a product defect or blocker, after a good-faith attempt on a supported browser.  
- Contact the email on your Stripe receipt to request a refund.  
- Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method via Stripe.

## What is not refundable under this short policy

- Change of mind after completing Cove Take and Harbor “felt that” experience, except as required by law.  
- Issues caused by unsupported devices or blocking extensions (we’ll still try to help).

## Cancellation

One-time founding purchases do **not** auto-renew. There is nothing to cancel.

## Legal

Local consumer law may provide additional rights; those rights take priority where applicable.
