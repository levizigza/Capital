# Scale gate (hard)

**No channel may be recommended for scale** until **all four** are evidenced for that channel’s cohort:

| Gate | Meaning (Capital) |
|------|-------------------|
| **1. Arrive** | Qualified ICP users land from the channel (screener pass) |
| **2. Activate** | Complete signature path beat: Cove Take → Harbor felt (or defined activation event) |
| **3. Retain** | Return day-2+ or complete a second session / next island intent |
| **4. WTP** | Behavioral or paid signal (purchase, preorder deposit, or unprompted paid-category spend proof + stated buy path)—not “I’d pay $X” vanity |

Until then: channels stay in **learn / manual** mode only.

Paid scalable recommendations are **out of scope for v0** except as a labeled future class.
