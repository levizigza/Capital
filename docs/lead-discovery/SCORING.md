# Lead score (0–100)

| Dimension | Max | Meaning |
|----------:|----:|---------|
| **Pain evidence** | 25 | Public signal they face kid/family money-teaching or youth fin-lit engagement problems |
| **ICP fit** | 20 | Match to S1 / S2 (primary) or S3 / S4 (adjacent) |
| **Timing** | 15 | Active program, recent news, mandate, Youth Month, convention cycle |
| **Ability to pay** | 20 | Is the **buyer** (budget owner), not only an end user |
| **Reachability** | 10 | Legitimate public access path (org email, form, partnership page) |
| **Strategic value** | 10 | Multiplier reach (coalition, convention, CU network) without requiring mass DM |

**Total = sum.** Qualified floor: **≥ 60**.

### Ability-to-pay clarity

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Buyer** | Controls program/curriculum/seat budget or household purchase |
| **Influencer** | Shapes adoption; may not sign the check |
| **User-only** | Experiences the problem; weak purchase authority — usually **disqualify** unless strategic research |

### Disqualify even if interesting

- No public access path  
- Pure competitor sales prospect with no problem-signal beyond “sells cards”  
- Private individuals without public professional role  
- Invented or guessed personal emails  

### Contact policy

Scores do **not** authorize outreach. See `OUTREACH_POLICY.md`.
