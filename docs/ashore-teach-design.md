## Honest bar

Ashore is **not** five slides. If a player notices “tutorial mode,” it failed.  
Iconic = one composition, body proves, almost no chrome, fantasy first.

## Laws

1. **One room, five proves** — Fantasy · Walk · Talk · Dock · Launch morph the **same** stage. No chamber title cards.
2. **Body proves** — Poke organ toy · claim walk rings · near+E Talk · board Cove carpet.
3. **One line of copy** visible at a time (whisper dock).
4. **Teach when needed** — Take · Plinth · Share · Soft Beat · Paycheck · Credit teach in the real world after earned.
5. **No map widen** — Harbor · Cove → Paycheck → Credit strip only.
6. **Leave · Esc** — veterans may skip; lands viable.
7. **Soundtrack stays** — organ stingers; opening bed.
8. **Sacred geometry** — Seed of Life rings **underfoot in the 3D pad**; no “Chamber 3/5” chrome.
