# Capital secret architecture — four layers

Secrets must **emerge from rules** (Takes, Soft Beats, weather, ledger, Family Room), not scavenger collectibles.  
Discovery rewards **knowledge · strategy · narrative · capability · new interactions** — never a checklist meter.

Freeze: Harbor · Cove → Paycheck → Credit. Family Room **local**. Quiet surface — no fake multiplayer mystery boards.

---

## Layers

| Level | Who finds it | How | Reward shape |
|-------|--------------|-----|--------------|
| **1 — Casual** | Anyone who plays the signature loop | Ambient plaza, Soft Beat exists, weather mood, day-2 echo, Outfitter/Capsule as discoveries | Narrative presence; world feels alive |
| **2 — Curious** | Players who experiment | Soft Beat after a Take; Teller with 2+ plaques; climb again | Fork vista (knowledge + story); first-peek thank-you |
| **3 — Expert** | Players who cross systems | Weather names organ ↔ scar; debt_fog rumor → Battlement; capsule on ledger Soft Beat; deal receipt on plaza; affinity Talk shelf | Strategy / capability / story bridges |
| **4 — Community** | Households sharing local myth | Family Room with ≥2 members + ≥2 plaques (JSON paste / same device) | Shared organ shelf only local myth can hold |

---

## Catalog (rule-emergent)

### Level 1 — naturally discovered
These are **not** “secrets” in a hunt sense — they are ambient truths the signature loop teaches without a tip sheet:

- Scar spectacle → Plinth → Harbor Felt share  
- Soft Beat pads exist inside Money Structures (Lid · Loft · Battlement · Teller)  
- Daily rumor / day-2 scar echo  
- Harbor weather fog when cashflow leans  
- Outfitter / Capsule Stall as lived places, not menu chrome  

### Level 2 — experimentation
- **Fork vista** — Lid/Loft/Battlement lines change after irreversible Take (`soft_beat_fork_vista`)  
- **Teller cross-index** — Memory Soft Beat with ≥2 plaques names Coin · Clock · Spiral together  
- Return peeks deepen copy without new maps; first peek may thank with a tiny coin (never gates Credit)

### Level 3 — interacting systems
- **Weather ↔ organ** — Daily Ritual / Capsule coach line cites scar organ + cashflow  
- **Debt fog → Battlement** — interest rumor foreshadows / confirms Spiral  
- **Capsule plaza** — Emergency Ledger / Bailout changes Teller whisper  
- **Deal receipt** — board assets echo on Freedom Pavilion  
- **Affinity shelf** — locals name your fork after repeated Talk  

### Level 4 — community-scale (local only)
- **Household organ shelf** — two plaques + two Family Room members → myth line only a shared room can speak  
- Export/import JSON carries the shelf; no server mystery  

---

## Anti-patterns (do not ship)

- Soft Beat completion meters / secret scavenger maps  
- Hidden main-quest islands beyond the triangle  
- Fake multiplayer ARG boards  
- Collectibles that don’t change a rule interaction
