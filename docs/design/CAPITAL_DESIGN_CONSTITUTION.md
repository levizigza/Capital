# Capital Design Constitution

**Status:** Durable principles for product, design, and engineering judgment  
**Mission:** Create an outstanding financial game whose gameplay itself develops better financial reasoning.  
**Derived from:** `docs/design/MASTER_DESIGN_AUDIT.md` · `docs/CAPITAL_DESIGN_BIBLE.md` · `docs/ftue/NORTH_STAR.md` · iconic freeze  
**Not:** A feature backlog. Principles outlive features. When a proposal conflicts with this constitution, change the proposal — or deliberately amend this document.

**How to use**

1. Read the mission and anti-pillars first.  
2. Score a proposal against the numbered principles.  
3. Prefer deepening interactions among existing primitives over adding systems.  
4. End with: *Does this help a player reason better about money through play — and remember how the world works?*

**Freeze (always on):** Main quest strip stays **Harbor · Cove → Paycheck → Credit**. Family Room stays **local**. No Nathan Project / BMO / CBE merges. Depth before map width.

## The one-sentence game

> In a world where money is alive, a washed-ashore Voyager learns that fortune is a journey of choices — until they escape paycheck-to-paycheck and return home transformed.

Craft compression: *I choose something about money → the living world marks it → Harbor remembers → I face the next living choice.*

## ~20 Durable principles

### 1. Interesting financial decisions over busywork

Prefer forks with tradeoffs (hold vs spend, wait vs haste, shelter vs pressure) over fetch, grind meters, login theater, or quiz chains that do not change the world.

**Test:** Would a thoughtful player pause? If not, it is busywork.

### 2. Causal clarity

Every major outcome must answer: *What did I do, and what did the world do because of it?* Take → hush → scar → Piggy naming the plaque is the gold standard. Silent meters and parallel “weather” fictions fail this principle.

**Test:** A cold player can retell the cause in one kid sentence.

### 3. Simple primitives, combinatorial depth

Keep a small set of living primitives — **Walk · Talk · Take · Soft Beat · Pay Day · Carpet · Structure · Scar / Plinth · Share** — and make them interact. Depth = cross-system consequences, not more HUD languages (XP, stance panels, skill bars, vanity seals).

**Test:** Can you name the primitive? If you need a new meter, you probably need a better interaction.

### 4. Consequential agency

Players commit; the world spends options. Irreversible Takes, cashflow footprints, weather, and trust matter. Soft gates may slow; they must never silently soft-lock travel or bury the next verb.

**Test:** The player can leave every screen and still feel authorship over money outcomes.

### 5. Learning through experience

Concepts arrive as situations in places — not worksheets with sprites. Teach order: situation → choice → response → consequence → understanding. Lectures, tip curricula, and “genre HUD syllabi” are last resorts.

**Test:** Could this beat exist with zero tutorial text and still teach something if the world reacts?

### 6. Independent transfer (king learning law)

After Capital teaches a principle once, players should correctly reason with it in a **substantially different** situation **without being told what to do**. Tutorial completion is diagnostic only. Independent Transfer Rate is the king learning KPI — never invent fake ITR numbers.

**Test:** Does this raise (or protect) transfer — or only completion?

### 7. Informative failure

Misses teach. Dignity overlays, Retry / stay-put, structure fails that stay in place, and scars that remain are required. Shame copy, dead ends, and “you are bad with money” framing are forbidden.

**Test:** After a fail, is the next verb clearer than before?

### 8. Contextual UI

Chrome appears when the place and beat need it. Quiet Harbor, cinema-hidden HUD, and one Coin Bag sentence beat dashboards. If removing a border/shadow/panel does not hurt understanding, remove it.

**Test:** Does this UI belong to *this* beat — or to a generic finance app?

### 9. Progressive disclosure

First hour: presence verbs (Walk · Talk · Carpet · Cove Take). Ledger, Freedom chase, Arcade, Studio, Ritual magnets, and side shores unlock as the world earns them — not as an options dump.

**Test:** Does the first viewport still feel like one composition and one next verb?

### 10. Player identity emerges from play

Identity is scars, weather, Soft Beats, Piggy lines, and Family myth — never an “I am a Saver” menu, archetype quiz as destiny, or stance HUD. Expression (carpet polish, Outfitter) must not outrank judgment.

**Test:** Can Harbor name who you became without opening a profile screen?

### 11. Mechanics and narrative coherence

If it ships on the spine, it must name a **money organ + suit verb** (Coin holds · Clock shelters · Spiral withstands · Memory keeps). Fantasy: Voyager among living money — not combat god, not spreadsheet operator.

**Test:** Mural law — organ + verb, or it does not ship on the spine.

### 12. One money truth

**One liquid pouch. One monthly cashflow engine. Memory (scars) as a stock.** Harbor sky/shops should speak cashflow. Do not run a second Harbor “economy phase” fiction beside CF. Pouch vanity never masquerades as Freedom.

**Test:** After a Take, can the player see *one* honest “what’s newly true about money?”

### 13. Depth before width

Deepen hush → scar spectacle → Plinth → share → Piggy → day-2 → Money Structure interiors before adding main-course islands or fake multiplayer. Side shores and toys stay optional.

**Test:** Does this create a new *decision* on the existing strip — or only a new pin on the map?

### 14. Accessibility is part of delight

Settings **or** OS reduced motion; damp signature flashes; readable text sizes; binding-aware hints; Esc + Leave on overlays; touch walk pads; mute that actually mutes. Soft-locks that depend on color, hearing, or perfect timing alone are bugs.

**Test:** Can someone finish the signature loop with volume at 0 and reduced motion on?

### 15. Ethical engagement

Respect attention and dignity. No dark patterns that trap, guilt-trip, or manufacture false urgency. Opt-in Talk. Navigability law: every overlay dismissible. Soft presence over ambush modals.

**Test:** Would you be comfortable watching a child play this beat?

### 16. No manipulative monetization

No pay-to-win. No real-money gates on learning, Freedom, or scars. In-world “shop” spends pouch coins only. Real checkout (if ever) stays out of iconic learning loop and never buys judgment outcomes.

**Test:** Can every learning-critical beat be completed without spending real money?

### 17. Measurable learning

Instrument what matters: Independent Transfer attempts/success, failure recovery, freeplay conversion, core-loop beats — with privacy-safe, id-only events. Prefer human playtests over vanity completion dashboards. Do not celebrate retention theater as proof of learning.

**Test:** Which learning metric moves, and how would we know we are wrong?

### 18. Measurable enjoyment

Enjoyment is felt in Commit juice, mute-test readability, share-worthiness of Harbor-felt cards, and willingness to return for Soft Beats / day-2 — not in XP toasts or streak guilt. Use playtests and cold retells; treat engagement metrics as investigations, not trophies.

**Test:** Would someone screenshot or retell this beat without being asked?

### 19. Technical reliability

Corrupt saves sanitize to playable Harbor. 3D failsafe → myth path with Talk + Carpet. Signature cinema must not soft-lock. Esc works. Pages deploy from known-good `main`. Performance budgets beat decorative particle spam.

**Test:** Poison the save and kill WebGL — can the player still sail?

### 20. Honest systems (no fake intelligence, no fake social)

Heuristics may nudge; generative “AI for AI’s sake” does not replace authored money situations. Family Room stays **local** — real household myth, not faux MMO. Do not invent backends, leaderboards, or NPC “friends” that pretend to be multiplayer.

**Test:** Are we honest about what the computer is doing — and what only a human can do?

### 21. Coach subordination

At most one clear next-verb coach in the first hour (Ashore + Piggy). Coin Bag points at Main Quest; adaptive tips never race spectacle, Take hush, or Plinth share. Discoveries (Outfitter, Capsule, Ritual) stay discoveries.

**Test:** Are two coaches arguing in the same viewport?

## Anti-pillars

**Capital is NOT:**

| Anti-pillar | Why it fails the mission |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| A quiz app with animations | Mastery quizzes may *gate*; they must not *be* the game. Learning lives in Takes, CF, and Harbor memory. |
| A spreadsheet simulator | CF is a living monthly engine felt in weather and Freedom — not a grid the player operates as their fantasy. |
| A casino | No random-pay dopamine replacing judgment; no loot that buys wisdom. |
| Pay-to-win | Real money must never purchase Freedom, scars, transfer success, or Ordeal outcomes. |
| AI for AI’s sake | No LLM tutor cosplay; authored situations + honest heuristics beat chatbot pedagogy. |
| A lecture disguised as gameplay | Tip walls, genre curricula, and tip-NPC syllabi are not Story Circles. |
| An XP treadmill | Silent or noisy level-ups that do not create new money decisions are hollow. |
| A map-expansion product | New main-course islands during iconic freeze dilute organs and transfer. |
| Fake multiplayer / global leaderboards | Retention theater; grief; breaks local Family Room honesty. |
| A combat RPG with coin skins | Talk is not HP duels; Voyagers choose, they do not DPS debt. |
| A settings onboarding void | Sterile chrome behind Talk fails fantasy and presence. |
| A dual-economy muddle | Two Harbor weathers / two “you’re winning” meters destroy causal clarity. |
| Shame pedagogy | Failures inform; they do not moralize poverty or spending. |
| Completionist Soft Beat bingo | Secrets from rules and curiosity — not “find all %” meters. |
| Engagement dark patterns | Streak guilt, soft-locks, undisableable overlays, false urgency. |
| Copycat design (“other games have this”) | Must pass fantasy + mural + transfer tests — not genre checklists. |

## Feature-approval checklist (constitution gate)

Before shipping on the spine, a change should clear:

- [ ] Creates or clarifies an **interesting financial decision**  
- [ ] Preserves **causal clarity** (player can retell cause → effect)  
- [ ] Uses **existing primitives** or justifies a new one against combinatorial depth  
- [ ] Protects **agency** and navigability (no soft-lock)  
- [ ] Serves **learning through experience** and **independent transfer**  
- [ ] Failures stay **informative and dignified**  
- [ ] UI is **contextual** with **progressive disclosure**  
- [ ] Identity stays **emergent** (no label menus)  
- [ ] Passes **mural law** (organ + suit verb) on spine  
- [ ] Honors **one money truth**  
- [ ] Respects **depth before width** freeze  
- [ ] Meets **accessibility** floor  
- [ ] Passes **ethical engagement** and **no manipulative monetization**  
- [ ] Names how **learning** and **enjoyment** would be observed  
- [ ] Does not reduce **technical reliability**  
- [ ] Avoids all **anti-pillars**

If more than two boxes fail — **do not ship**; deepen an existing interaction instead.
