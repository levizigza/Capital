# Capital chase target — decision-making under constraints

**Status:** Canonical product direction  
**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Law:** Do not bolt financial literacy onto a videogame. Make the financial literacy **be** the gameplay.

---

## The insight

**Capital’s real subject is not money.**  
**It is decision-making under constraints.**

Money is the **medium**.

Liquidity, investing, debt, risk, careers, business, housing, and inflation are different **environments** in which the player confronts the same deeper questions:

| Deeper question | Player-facing shape |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| What do I want? | Goal / desire / organ taste |
| What information do I have? | Visible ledger, weather, scars, Soft Beat peeks |
| What am I giving up? | Tradeoff on the Take / deal / liability fork |
| What might happen? | Probable / unknown consequences |
| How much uncertainty can I tolerate? | Wait vs haste · hold vs spend · shelter vs pressure |
| What does this decision make possible—or impossible—later? | Scar · CF · weather · Freedom · next door |

If Capital makes **that** genuinely fun, literacy does not need a worksheet skin.  
**The financial literacy is the gameplay.**

That is the version of Capital we chase.

---

## One-sentence chase

> A Voyager learns to decide under living money constraints — what they want, what they know, what they give up, what might happen, how much uncertainty they can bear, and what their choice locks in later — until Harbor remembers who they became.

Craft compression (unchanged):  
*I choose something about money → the living world marks it → Harbor remembers → I face the next living choice.*

## Money as medium (not the syllabus)

| Environment (later content) | Constraint pressure it teaches |
|----------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Liquidity / paycheck | Timing, buffer, want vs need under thin cash |
| Investing | Uncertainty, opportunity cost, delayed consequence |
| Debt / credit | Path dependence — what this makes impossible later |
| Risk | Tolerance for unknown outcomes |
| Careers / business | Income stream as a decision object |
| Housing | Long lock-in vs flexibility |
| Inflation | Rules changing under you |

Do **not** unlock these as a curriculum checklist. Unlock them as **new constraint environments** for the same decision questions — after the spine proves the questions are fun.

Freeze still holds: deepen **Harbor · Cove → Paycheck → Credit** before any map widen.

## Implementation order (actually use this)

Do **not** start with: AI guide · story engine · social features · giant financial simulation.

### Sequence

```text
1  Audit
2  Constitution
3  Decisions
4  Core loop
5  Playable finance
6  System depth
8  Feedback
9  UI
13 Learning transfer
19 Measurement
21 Real users
```

That yields:

**Audit → constitution → decisions → core loop → playable finance → system depth → feedback → UI → learning transfer → measurement → real users.**

Then — and only then — we earn the right to add complexity.

| Deferred | Why wait |
|----------|----------|
| **7 / AI guide** | Intervention only after transfer + feedback are honest; never answer-coach |

Numbers skipped in the chase order (7, 10–12, 14–18, 20, …) are **not abandoned forever** — they are **gated** behind proving steps 1→21’s product is fun.

## Design tests (every proposal)

1. **Constraint test:** Does this force a tradeoff under limited information, options, or time?  
2. **Question test:** Does the player confront at least one of the six deeper questions?  
3. **Medium test:** Is money the *pressure*, not a quiz skin or XP faucet?  
4. **Transfer test:** Could the same judgment skill show up in a different money environment later?  
5. **Fun test:** Would a skilled player still care after the authored spine — without grind?  
6. **Order test:** Are we jumping ahead of Audit→…→Real users into AI/story/social/giant-sim?

Fail any of 1–3 → redesign. Fail 6 → put it back in the deferred bin.

## Relationship to quality formula

```text
CAPITAL QUALITY ≈
  INTERESTING FINANCIAL DECISIONS
× UNDERSTANDABLE CAUSALITY
× PLAYER AGENCY
× TRANSFERABLE LEARNING
× EMOTIONAL MEANING
× SYSTEM DEPTH
× FRICTIONLESS UX
× TRUST
```

“Interesting financial decisions” means **interesting decisions under financial constraints** — not interesting *about* money trivia.

Raising one dimension must not trash another (`QUALITY_NORTH_STAR_AUDIT.md`).

## Bottom line

Chase the version where:

- **Subject** = decision-making under constraints  
- **Medium** = living money  
- **Literacy** = the gameplay itself  
- **Order** = Audit → constitution → decisions → core loop → playable finance → system depth → feedback → UI → learning transfer → measurement → real users  
- **Complexity** = earned after that sequence holds  

Do not start with the AI guide, the story engine, social features, or a giant financial simulation.
