# Skilled player at 100 hours — Capital

**Lens:** A Voyager who has cleared the iconic spine (Ashore → Cove → Paycheck → Freedom Seal → Credit), sampled sides, shared a card, named Family Room — and kept playing.  
**Session truth:** Capital is a **short iconic spine** (often 10–30 min sessions), not a 100h campaign. A 100h life only stays alive if **existing systems keep producing new situations**.  
**Law:** Do **not** solve longevity by adding grind (XP sinks, carpet coin walls, login streaks, battle pass, more main-course islands). Prefer mastery · combination · uncertainty · expression · local social · emergence.

**Canon:** `docs/LONGEVITY_100H.md` · `docs/iconic-path.md` · freeze Cove → Paycheck → Credit + Harbor; Family Room local.  
**Sibling:** `docs/design/FAKE_MECHANICS_AUDIT.md` (extrinsic scaffolding).

### Early-solve law

If a system is **solved before it teaches judgment**, either:

1. **Replace** it with a contextual decision (transfer proof, liability fork, regenerating deal tradeoffs), or  
2. **Accept** it as spine scaffolding and stop using it as the 100h hook.

Never extend solved systems with more time-to-complete.

## Bottom line

At 100 hours, Capital is either:

1. **A solved museum** of three beautiful Takes and a coin faucet — or  
2. **A living Harbor** where old scars still change Soft Beats, weather, deals, and what a sibling stamps on your share.

Grind builds (1). Combination · judgment · local social · emergence build (2).

Prefer (2). Keep the Plinth true. Make tomorrow remember yesterday — differently. Do not solve longevity by adding grind.
