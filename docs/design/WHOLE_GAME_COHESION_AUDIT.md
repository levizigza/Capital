## Iconic close plan (one cosmos)

The bar: after a cold play, a kid (or a tired adult) can say **four sentences** and mean them:

- The Coin holds — I put some in the jar.
- The Clock shelters — I paid the rain before glitter.
- The Spiral withstands — I waited instead of grabbing.
- Memory keeps — Harbor still knows.

Then, without a coach, they face a **new stall** and choose anyway (**ITR**).

### Sequence of work (no map width)

1. **Prove the peak with humans** — Pattern #94 + ITR n≥5. Fix whatever they actually misunderstand.
2. **One front door** — collapse Ashore vs Piggy duplication; hide series leads on first meet; in-world Harbor brief.
3. **Protect transfer** — lock Paycheck until Cove Change; delay era shores until after Paycheck (or Credit).
4. **One Harbor verb between paintings** — Living Cashflow Commit *or* a quieter Memory afterglow, not both plus Arcade plus eight shores.
5. **Tell the truth about Freedom vs Spiral** — one mythology, kid copy, no quiz ghosts.
6. **Unify leftover voices** — Piggy gossip, pad toys, one weather, one scoreboard.
7. **Master of Two Worlds** — a post-Credit Harbor that feels like Change, not a shop.

Until 1–3 land, further content is **more glue**, not more icon.
