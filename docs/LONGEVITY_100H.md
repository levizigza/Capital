# Longevity at 100 hours — mastery, not grind

**Lens:** A player who has lived in Capital for ~100 hours — triangle Clears done, Freedom Seal earned, Credit Ordeal finished, side shores sampled, Outfitter full, share card sent, Family Room named.

**Law:** Do **not** fix longevity by adding grind, XP sinks, more main-course islands, or fake online retention. Longevity = **mastery · interaction · uncertainty · expression · social dynamics · emergence**.

**Canon:** [iconic-path.md](./iconic-path.md) · [iconic-later.md](./iconic-later.md) · [player-fantasy-and-loop.md](./player-fantasy-and-loop.md) · freeze: Harbor · Cove → Paycheck → Credit; Family Room local.  
**Sibling audits:** complexity cut · replayability · social · identity (branch docs when present) — this file is the 100h player lens.

**Honest frame:** Capital’s iconic bar is a **short unforgettable spine**, not a 100h campaign. Session targets in [game-pillars.md](./game-pillars.md) are 10–30 minutes. A 100h life only stays alive if **existing systems keep producing new situations** — not if we stretch the same Take three times.

---

## Portrait: the 100h Voyager

They can cold-retell Coin · Clock · Spiral · Memory. They know the Plinth glow. They have a favorite scar. Harbor weather still flickers when cashflow slips. The deal catalog is owned. Carpet vanity thresholds are a coin faucet. Party board is a known 15-space rhythm. Soft Beats are four poems they can recite.

They are not bored of *Capital’s fantasy*. They are bored of **solved meters** and **authored content with no new forks**.

## What new situations can existing systems produce?

Favor **combinatorics** over new content packs:

```
Take residue → Ledger (asset/liability)
     ↓
Pay Day / CF streak
     ↓
Harbor weather + shop prices
     ↓
Soft Beat / Piggy / day-2 reading of scar
     ↓
(optional) next board bias / Family myth
```

| Existing pair | New situation if deepened |
|---------------|---------------------------|
| Scar × Soft Beat | Fork-true vistas (same pad, different world) |
| Scar × Pay Day | Spender/haste leave ledger residue |
| CF × Weather × Shop | Already live — teach as mastery, don’t add second weather |
| Board × economy phase | Space-kind bias (more bills in recession) |
| Lucky/capsule/liability × choice | Spend/bank, pick-1-of-2, borrow/buyout/walk |
| Plaque × Family Room × share | Human judgment loop |
| Soft Beat × next Take | Prep the emotional “why” before Paycheck/Credit |

This is the longevity engine. **Not** +30 daily quests.

## Longevity proposals (no grind)

Each proposal multiplies **existing** systems. Depth tag = strategic richness for a 100h player.

### Mastery

| Proposal | Depth | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Soft Beat **fork vistas** (scar-aware) | ↑ | Same pad; different moral weather — no new island |
| Soft Beat **arms next Pay Day** (tiny bias) | ↑ | Peek → consequence; still one conversation |
| Liability land = **borrow / buyout / walk** | ↑ | Informed fork every board storm |
| Teach weather as **readable skill** (Piggy names storm causes) | ↑ | Mastery of what already exists |

### Interaction

| Proposal | Depth | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Take → **ledger residue** (spender/haste) | ↑ | Runs diverge after same graph |
| Chain detection: binge → streak break → storm → Soft Beat line | ↑ | Emergence without new meter |
| Unify sky on **CF weather only** (keep macro boom out of Harbor Pay Day) | →/↑ | Clarity = deeper reading |

### Uncertainty

| Proposal | Depth | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Session **board pattern rotation** | ↑ | Same rules, different pressure |
| Economy phase **biases space kinds** | ↑ | Recession week feels different |
| Lucky: **spend all vs bank half** | ↑ | Randomness pressures a choice |
| Capsule: **pick 1 of 2** | ↑ | |
| Regenerating / rotating deals after catalog clear | ↑ | **Not** higher prices — new tradeoffs |

### Expression

| Proposal | Depth | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Emergent **archetype naming** (Piggy/weather/Plinth) | ↑ | Saver · Hasteheart · Stormdock — earned titles, not XP ranks |
| Share card variants from **live weather + scar** | ↑ | Same PNG system, new truth |
| Companions stay cosmetic | → | Never gate CF |

### Social dynamics (local only)

| Proposal | Depth | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| **Family Challenge** (one household goal) | ↑ | Human picks meaning |
| **Share Witness** stamps | ↑ | Inbound judgment on a Take |
| Hot-seat **rival seat** on board forks | ↑ | Spite CPU can’t fake |
| Reject: global leaderboards, online trade, alliances | — | Retention theater + freeze break |

### Emergence

| Proposal | Depth | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Voyage Log of **detected chains** (not quest log) | ↑ | Story from rules |
| Debt retirement as mid/late decision | ↑ | CF puzzle returns after “solved” assets |
| Second profile **contrast shelf** on Plinth | ↑ | Your saver vs spender twin |

## Anti-patterns (will not count as longevity)

- More Islands XP / battle pass / daily login coins  
- Widening main strip beyond Cove → Paycheck → Credit  
- Fake multiplayer backend or global rankings  
- Longer Soft Beat text / more tip NPCs  
- Inflating Freedom target as “endgame grind”  
- Vanity carpet coin walls as the 100h hook  
- New meters that don’t change Take · Soft Beat · scar · CF · Plinth within a session  

If a proposal only adds **time**, cut it. If it adds **judgment**, keep it.

## Relationship to complexity cut

When a complexity-cut review is present, it removes **hollow surface**. This doc says what to **do with the attention you free**: deepen interactions among Take · Soft Beat · CF · weather · Plinth · Family Room.

Cut affinity/XP chrome → spend craft on fork vistas and local social judgment.  
Fewer systems, richer interactions — that **is** the 100h plan.

---

## Bottom line

At 100 hours, Capital is either:

1. **A solved museum** of three beautiful Takes and a coin faucet — or  
2. **A living Harbor** where old scars still change Soft Beats, weather, deals, and what a sibling stamps on your share.

Grind builds (1). Mastery · interaction · uncertainty · expression · local social · emergence build (2).

Prefer (2). Keep the Plinth true. Make tomorrow remember yesterday — differently.
