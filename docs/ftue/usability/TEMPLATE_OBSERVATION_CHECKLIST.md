# Observer pocket checklist — record lenses

Keep beside the session log. Tick during play; expand quotes in the log.

**Participant:** P___ · **Cohort:** ___ · **Build:** ___

## Record

- [ ] What player **notices**
- [ ] What player **ignores**
- [ ] What they **expect**
- [ ] What they **attempt**
- [ ] **Why they think** an outcome occurred (their words)
- [ ] Where they **hesitate**
- [ ] Where they **fail**
- [ ] Whether they **recover** (self / intervention / no · attempts: __)
- [ ] What **strategy** they form
- [ ] Whether they **transfer** learning (success / fail / not reached)
- [ ] What they choose during **free play**

## Also

- [ ] Help asks + scripted response / INTERVENTION
- [ ] Believed change vs **actually changed** (verify after)
- [ ] Contaminated? No / Yes — reason: _______________

## After session → finding cards

Each sticky issue gets:

`OBSERVATION` → `INTERPRETATION` → `HYPOTHESIS` → `RECOMMENDATION`  
(+ Evidence · Severity)
